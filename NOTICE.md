- The kit works on sites with your own login (Instagram, X, Threads, LinkedIn, YouTube Studio). You are responsible for following each site's rules. Keep the pace human: the comment role waits 15 to 30 seconds between replies.
- Selectors for these sites change. When a role stops finding a button, it stops and says so instead of guessing.
- No data leaves your machine through this kit except what you approve to post.
